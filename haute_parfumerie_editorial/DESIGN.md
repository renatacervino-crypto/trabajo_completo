## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory intimacy and quiet opulence of an archival French fragrance house tailored for modern digital commerce. Designed for an audience of discerning perfume collectors, aesthetic purists, and luxury gift-seekers, the interface communicates restraint, rare craftsmanship, and timeless provenance.

The design movement synthesizes **Refined Editorial Minimalism** with tactile haute-couture publication cues. Rather than relying on overt decorative flourishes, the system leverages generous, rhythmic whitespace, asymmetric editorial composition, razor-thin metallic hairline dividers, and deliberate typographic pacing. Micro-interactions are deliberate and unhurried—characterized by soft easing, nuanced opacity shifts, and tactile feedback reminiscent of heavy unbleached paper stock and cold-stamped gold foil.

## Colors

The palette mimics raw, noble materials: unglazed porcelain, woven linen, aged amber resins, and hand-cast gold.

- **Primary (`#1A1817`)**: Deep Onyx. Used for authoritative headline typography, primary call-to-action buttons, high-contrast framing, and focused iconography.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted Champagne Gold. Acts as an elevated accent for micro-borders, category tags, scent notes, active indicators, and gilded hover interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#9B7E51`)**: Antique Amber/Burnished Bronze. Reserved for contextual emphasis, botanical profile callouts, artisanal badges, and active state feedback.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5`)**: Warm Alabaster Canvas. Forms the foundation of the interface. Paired with Ivory Stone (`#F5F2EB`) for nested containers, card backdrops, and product staging platters.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E8E4DC`)**: Warm Taupe. Used strictly at 1px thickness for structural delineation, grid lines, and list dividers without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence reminiscent of archival monographs and high-fashion journals.

- **Playfair Display** commands editorial presence across hero spaces, fragrance titles, olfactory chapter headings, and quotes. It is tuned with refined line heights and slightly compressed letter-spacing on larger sizes to evoke high-fashion print layouts.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces neutral, pristine legibility for utilitarian tasks, product specifications, prices, ingredients, and micro-copy. 
- **The Caps Tracked Hierarchy (`label-caps`)**: All section overlines, scent note categories (Head, Heart, Base), volume specifications (e.g., `100 ML · EAU DE PARFUM`), and navigational tags must be rendered in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.15em`) to evoke fragrance bottle label engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an editorial 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around asymmetry and negative space.

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid, dynamic maximum width of 1440px, `margin-desktop` (64px / 4rem), `gutter-desktop` (40px / 2.5rem). Product showcases, fragrance narrative cards, and olfactory pyramids alternate between wide 7-column feature blocks and narrow 5-column descriptor stacks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with 32px gutters and 32px margins. Two-column product tiles with generous 48px vertical gaps.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column grid with `margin` (20px / 1.25rem) and `gutter` (24px / 1.5rem). Single-column flow with full-bleed imagery offset by inset typographic cards.

Vertical spacing adheres strictly to open, breathing rhythms. Section breaks leverage large vertical cushions (`space-xl` scaled to double across viewports) to ensure each formulation and visual element functions as a standalone gallery exhibit.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy synthetic drop shadows, skeuomorphic bevels, and blurry glassmorphism in favor of **Tonal Layering and Warm Hairline Definition**.

- **Architectural Flatness**: Surfaces maintain tactile closeness to paper. Elevation is primarily expressed by alternating background tonal planes (`#FAF8F5` base against `#F5F2EB` surface containers).
- **Hairline Framing**: Visual boundaries use 1px solid borders in Stone Taupe (`#E8E4DC`) or soft Champagne Gold (`#C5A880` at 30%–60% opacity) rather than casting shadows.
- **Ambient Fragrance Glow (Floating Flyouts and Overlays)**: For floating flyout carts, boutique filters, and sensorial modals, depth is achieved through an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.03);`
  - Inset micro-borders (`1px solid #FAF8F5`) layered on top of containers provide a crisp, polished edge.

## Shapes

The foundational shape language is **Architectural & Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**, reflecting the pristine silhouette of cut glass flacons, bespoke packaging cartons, and heavy paper stock.

- **Panels, Cards, and Modals**: Rendered with absolute 90-degree corners (`border-radius: 0px`) to preserve structural poise and editorial rigor.
- **The Perfume Pill Exception**: A specific exception is made for small informational taxonomy tokens, volume selectors, and botanical note tags. These employ a full pill radius (`9999px`), contrasting the razor-sharp rectilinear canvas with a shape inspired by classic French apothecary seals and oval fragrance labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp corners (`0px`), Deep Onyx background (`#1A1817`), Warm Alabaster text (`#FAF8F5`), `label-caps` typography. Minimum height 52px, padding `0 2.5rem`. On hover, transitions seamlessly to Antique Amber (`#9B7E51`) with a subtle 200ms ease.
- **Secondary Action**: Sharp corners (`0px`), transparent background, 1px solid Deep Onyx border (`#1A1817`), Deep Onyx text. On hover, fills with Deep Onyx while text shifts to Alabaster.
- **Tertiary / Text Link**: Unbordered, `label-caps` styling with a persistent 1px hairline underline in Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) offset by 6px.

### Olfactory Note & Taxonomy Chips
- Oval pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), 1px solid `#E8E4DC`, unselected background `#FAF8F5`.
- Typography: `label-sm` with subtle letter-spacing.
- Active / Selected State: Border transitions to `#C5A880`, background shifts to `#F5F2EB`, text color darkens to `#1A1817`.

### Product Cards
- Background: `#F5F2EB`. Sharp boundaries with an optional 1px `#E8E4DC` frame.
- Image staging area maintains a 4:5 vertical portrait aspect ratio with generous internal whitespace around the flacon.
- Content stack: Uppercase collection kicker (`label-caps`), fragrance title in `headline-md`, concise olfactory notes (e.g., *Bergamot, Iris Concrete, Smoked Cedar*), followed by discrete price presentation in `label-md`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Minimalist underline or boxed hairline: 1px border in `#E8E4DC` against `#FAF8F5`.
- Height: 48px with generous side padding (`space-md`).
- Focus state: Border smoothly shifts to Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) with zero glow or outer ring. Labels animate upward into `label-caps` in `#9B7E51`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes: 16px sharp squares (`0px` radius) with 1px `#1A1817` border when checked and custom brass check icon.
- Radios: 16px concentric circles; active state displays a solid `#1A1817` center dot suspended within a `#C5A880` circular track.

### Olfactory Accord Pyramid (Custom Component)
- A specialized three-tier architectural component (Top, Heart, Base Notes) aligned vertically with 1px gold micro-rules separating each tier. Displays botanical origin badges and accord weight indicators via micro-proportional bars.
